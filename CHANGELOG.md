# Changelog

What changed in each version of Ondu for Android, newest first. A version's section becomes
the notes of its GitHub release, so write it for the people installing it.

## [0.1.0] - Unreleased

The first test build: the Android version of Ondu, with the iOS app's features.

- Sign in to your Ondu server and sync your library: artists, albums, songs and playlists.
- Play with gapless albums, crossfade, volume normalisation, shuffle and repeat, with
  controls on the lock screen, in the notification shade and on headphones and Bluetooth.
- Download songs, albums, artists and playlists to listen offline.
- Create, edit and organise playlists and folders, also while offline.
- Search the library, with recent searches.
- Colours from your wallpaper or a choice of accents, and a player tinted by the album
  artwork.
